About

Another Human in the Loop is a creative laboratory exploring the space between human intention, computational systems, and emergent behavior.

I'm Justin Lincoln, an artist, educator, designer, and creative technologist working with generative art, creative coding, interaction, sound, and emerging technologies.

My work often begins with a simple system and then asks what happens when I give that system room to behave on its own. The resulting experiments can become images, animations, instruments, interfaces, or interactive environments.

Practice

Processing, p5.js, shaders, sound, interaction design, generative systems, and experiments with AI.
